Scope and data

This notice covers configuration, non-binding quotation requests, orders, subscriptions, support and withdrawal. We process your name or business name, email, country of residence or business establishment (including the country name entered for international enquiries), optional phone number, plan, scope quantities, selected modules, amounts or estimates, request status and choices with document versions and timestamps. Further tax details are requested only when needed for a subsequent quotation or invoicing. Do not enter passwords, OTPs, private keys, scanner content or third-party personal data in the configurator. Selected goals are recorded in the order or quotation request using category identifiers to prepare the requested service; no personal inventories, credentials or free-text descriptions of your circumstances are collected here.

Purposes and legal bases

Configuration and quotation enquiries initiated by the individual, requested pre-contractual steps, conclusion and performance of the contract, access, service communications and support: GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Tax, accounting and consumer-rights obligations: Article 6(1)(c). Evidence of the relationship or enquiry, claims and abuse prevention: the controller’s legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f), subject to necessity and balancing of rights. Email marketing: separate, optional and withdrawable consent under Article 6(1)(a), without affecting the service or enquiry. Consent is not required for data necessary to handle requested pre-contractual steps or the contract.

Required information and choices

Name, email, country and configuration are needed to handle an order or quotation request; for a country other than Italy, its name must be entered. Phone number and marketing are optional. An international enquiry requires acknowledgment of the privacy notice, not acceptance of a contract or an early-start request. Risk Snapshot answers are processed in your browser and are not sent through the new order or enquiry flow. The plan recommendation is indicative and can be changed. We do not make legally significant decisions solely through automated profiling.

Recipients

The controller, authorised staff and administrative advisers access information only as necessary. Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and email providers support registration and communications. A quotation request is stored in AETERNA’s sheet and emailed to the requester and the controller for handling; it is not a promotional communication. Banks, tax advisers and authorities receive the information needed for their roles. The hosting website, GitHub Pages and, when embedded, Google Sites may process technical connection data under their own notices. We do not sell or publicly disclose your data.

International providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, transfers rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses and any additional safeguards required by GDPR. Contact the controller for details and copies of relevant safeguards, recipients and locations. Administrative access must be restricted to authorised people. We do not promise zero risk or exclusively European storage.

Retention

Subscription data are retained during the relationship. Tax and contractual records and order evidence are retained as necessary for legal duties and legal claims, ordinarily up to 10 years after the relationship ends or the document date, subject to disputes or different statutory periods. Support messages unnecessary for those purposes: up to 24 months after closing the request. Marketing: until consent withdrawal or a maximum of 24 months after consent, with a new choice before continuing. Minimal evidence of withdrawal may remain to honour it. Clearing the page does not delete registered orders, emails or downloaded files. A zero-retention philosophy for analysis content does not remove contractual retention duties; this page does not certify other scanners. Quotation enquiries that do not lead to a contract are treated as service communications: up to 24 months after the request is closed, except for data needed for legal duties or legal claims. Registering an enquiry does not turn it into an order; clearing the page does not delete registered requests or emails.
