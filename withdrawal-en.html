Deadline

Consumers may withdraw without giving reasons within 14 days of contract conclusion, meaning confirmed order registration. Sending the notice before the deadline is sufficient. Statutory extensions and protections for missing information remain applicable.

How to withdraw

Email or write to the seller, or use “Withdraw from the contract here” in the configurator footer. Enter your name, contract reference and confirmation email, then select “Confirm withdrawal”. No reason or customer login is required. An acknowledgment containing your statement, date and time is provided on a durable medium without undue delay; a submission record can be downloaded. If online submission is unconfirmed, also email us without waiting.

Refund

We refund the amount due without undue delay and within 14 days of your notice, using the original payment method unless expressly agreed otherwise, without refund fees. For bank transfers we may request only the details needed to refund you. Following an express early-start request, a proportionate amount for services actually supplied may be payable where legally permitted. Without such a request, early performance does not create a charge in the circumstances provided by law.
